Common questions

How is overtime pay calculated?
Overtime is your base hourly rate multiplied by a penalty rate for the extra hours — commonly time-and-a-half (1.5x) for the first few overtime hours and double time (2x) after that. Multiply the hours in each band by the rate and add them up.
What are penalty rates?
Penalty rates are higher rates for hours worked at less sociable times — evenings, weekends and public holidays. Typical examples are 1.5x on Saturdays, 2x on Sundays and 2.5x on public holidays, though the exact rates depend on your award or agreement.
Does casual loading stack with penalty rates?
Usually the casual loading (commonly 25%) is added to the base rate, and penalty rates then apply — but how they combine varies by award. Turn on the casual option to include the loading, and check your specific award for how they interact.
Is overtime taxed more?
No. Overtime is taxed at your normal marginal rate like the rest of your pay. It can look heavily taxed on a single payslip because a big week pushes up that pay period's withholding, but it evens out across the year.
