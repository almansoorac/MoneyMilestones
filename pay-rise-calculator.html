Common questions

Will a pay rise push me into a higher bracket and leave me worse off?
No. Australia's tax is marginal — only the portion of income above each threshold is taxed at the higher rate. A pay rise always leaves you with more in your pocket, just not the full gross amount.
Why do I keep so little of my pay rise?
Because the rise sits on top of your existing income, it is taxed at your top marginal rate, plus Medicare and any HECS repayment. That effective rate is higher than your average tax rate on your whole salary.
Does HECS reduce my pay rise?
It can. Because HECS repayments rise with income, a pay rise can increase your compulsory repayment — and crossing a repayment threshold can take a noticeable extra slice.
How is the effective rate on my rise calculated?
We compare your take-home before and after the rise and express the difference kept as a percentage of the gross increase. The rest is your effective rate on the additional income.
