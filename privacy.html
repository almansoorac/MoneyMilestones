Privacy Policy

How MoneyMilestones handles information when you use this site.

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you visit moneymilestones.com.au, and how advertising and cookies work on the site. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

MoneyMilestones is a static website. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't ask for personal details to use the calculators. The numbers you type into a calculator are processed entirely in your own browser to produce a result — they are not sent to us or stored on our servers.

Like most websites, our hosting and security infrastructure may automatically log standard technical information such as browser type, device and approximate region, purely to serve the site reliably and securely. This isn't used to identify you personally.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertising, which keeps the tools free to use. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

For more on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's policies.

Analytics

We may use aggregate, privacy-respecting analytics and Google Search Console to understand how the site is found and used — for example, which guides are popular. These measure trends in aggregate and are not used to identify individual visitors.

Links to other sites

Our guides may link to external sites such as government resources. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we'd encourage you to read their own policies.

Children

This site is intended for a general adult audience making financial decisions and is not directed at children.

Your choices and contact

You can control cookies through the opt-out links above and your browser settings. If you have any questions about this policy or how information is handled, please contact us at fahad.mofah@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

General information only — not financial, tax or legal advice. Our guides and calculators are educational tools and reflect the 2026-27 rules; whether they apply depends on your circumstances.
